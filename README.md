# Scrib — free beta downloads

**Scrib** is the notepad for Windows that keeps up: plain-text notes that turn
into checklists, running totals, tables and split bills as you type, text
pulled from screenshots, timers, instant search, and 24 themes. No account,
and your notes stay on your computer.

**Scrib is free while it's in beta. If it ever becomes paid, the version you
have keeps working.**

## Download

Go to [Releases](https://github.com/CydoEntis/scrib-releases/releases/latest)
and pick your file:

- **Windows 10 and 11:** `Scrib-setup.exe`. Windows may say "Windows protected
  your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet: click **More info**, then
  **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** the `.AppImage` (make it executable with `chmod +x`, then run
  it), the `.deb` for Ubuntu and Debian, or the `.rpm` for Fedora.
  Screenshot text capture works on Windows only.

Scrib checks here once a day for a newer version and asks before installing it.

you did, what you expected, and what happened. Please don't paste private
notes.

More at [getscrib.com](https://getscrib.com/).
